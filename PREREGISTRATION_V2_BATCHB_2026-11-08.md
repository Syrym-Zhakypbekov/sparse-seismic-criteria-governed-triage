## 4. Hypotheses
**Primary of batch B (pooled over the included batch-B networks).** Exactly V1 and co-primary V1r of the batch-A plan
(CG rule v2 non-inferior to P3 7.5 dB, margin 3 pp on the upper 95 % bound of the pooled difference in dismissed share; ratio
(CG + 0.5) / (P3 + 0.5) upper 95 % bound <= 1.5), two-stage cluster bootstrap (networks, then days with episodes), 2000 resamples,
seed 7. Secondary V2-V8 of the batch-A plan, on the batch-B pooled set, unchanged.
**Per network (secondary).** V1 per batch-B network with the day bootstrap; Holm step-down across the included batch-B networks at
one-sided alpha 0.025; unadjusted results also reported.
**Combined A+B (secondary).** The same V1, V1r and V2-V8 on all included networks of batches A and B together (same bootstrap),
and Holm across all included A+B networks. The batch-A primary remains the batch-A primary; batch B does not replace it, and a
combined result is never reported instead of the separate ones.

## 5. Compute order and stopping
- Networks are started in rule_order (11, 12, ...), as many in parallel as the fleet allows; the platform per network is logged in
  EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md and does not change code or parameters.
- A batch-B network whose processing has not finished by 05:00 Almaty (00:00 UTC) on 08.11.2026 is reported as
  "not completed (compute)", with the chunks that finished described but not scored. It is never dropped silently and never
  replaced; networks later in the order are not started in its place after the deadline. The batch-B pooled analysis uses the
  batch-B networks completed and included under the exclusion rules.
- No network is added, dropped or reordered after this commit. Analysis is run once per mode (B, AB) by the pushed scorer after
  every batch-B network has finished, been declared not run, or been declared not completed (compute). A scorer bug found later is
  fixed in a separate logged commit with before/after output kept.

## 6. Reported whatever the outcome
All batch-B and combined hypotheses are reported, including failures, non-exercised ones and networks not completed. No threshold,
weight, margin, D, network, order or period is changed after data are seen.
