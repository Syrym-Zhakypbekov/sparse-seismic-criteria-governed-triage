# Plan (post hoc, method fixed before computing): forward-in-time re-tuning, low-chance subset, G.WUS removal

Written 2026-11-08, before any of the three analyses below was computed. Answers the v32 internal review:
reviewer points (inverted temporal split / re-tune forward in time; chance-dominated networks) and point m9 (G.WUS).
No new data, no change to pipeline/. Bootstrap: two-stage cluster bootstrap of analysis/v2_networks.cluster_boot
(networks, then days; 2000 resamples; seed 7); margin 3 percentage points; ratio criterion 1.5 (+0.5 continuity).
All three are post hoc and reported as such, whatever they show.

## G1. Forward-in-time re-tuning (33 reviewed-label networks, results/posthoc/_collect_reviewed.pkl)

- Split per network: the EARLY window is the first 30 calendar days that contain episodes; the TEST window is every later day.
- Re-tuning uses only the early windows of all 33 networks, pooled (one global operating point per policy, as in the frozen design).
- Operator target fixed now: the dismissal threshold of each policy is the most aggressive value whose pooled early-window share
  of reviewed-associated alarms dismissed is at most T = 3.0 % (the frozen P3 rate over the whole period is 3.49 %).
  - P3: SNR threshold t on a grid 5.0-12.0 dB in steps of 0.25 (t is used for alerting and dismissing, as frozen).
  - CG (c = 1), CG c = 0.5, CG-D: theta_D on a grid 0.04-0.40 in steps of 0.01.
  - theta_A (CG family): the lowest value on 0.40-0.90 (step 0.01, above theta_D) whose pooled early-window alert precision
    (share of automatic alerts that are reviewed-associated) is at least that of P3 at its re-tuned t.
  - delta and the weights stay frozen.
- Test (later days only): each CG variant minus P3 (both re-tuned), pooled difference with interval, ratio, unassociated alerts,
  escalations. Reported against the same comparison with the frozen thresholds restricted to the same test days.
- Questions: do the verdicts (margin 3) and the ordering of dismissals c = 1 >= CG-D, c = 0.5 hold forward in time?

## G2. Low-chance subset

- Chance share per network = chance_control.chance_associated_mean / associated, from results/v2_reviewed/v2_eval_reviewed.json.
- Pre-set threshold: share <= 0.30. Pooled reviewed results (CG, CG-D, CG c = 0.5 vs P3) on that subset, and on its complement.

## G3. G.WUS removal (point m9)

- Re-score E2 (IU.MAKZ extended, analysis/extended_eval.py logic) and K2 (II.KURK extended, analysis/kurk_extended.py logic)
  with every G.WUS state set to MISSING, for all policies; report unassociated alerts (E2/K2) and dismissed associated alarms.
- Not done here: a reviewer's per-neighbour chance-YES rate from time-shifted neighbour triggers (needs trigger times not kept in the
  episode ledgers).

Outputs: results/gap2/forward_retune.json, results/gap2/low_chance.json, results/gap2/wus_removed.json; script analysis/gap2.py.
